Who We Are

A sanctuary built
for your beauty.

Vie is more than a salon — it's a destination. When you step through our doors in the heart of Salmiya, you leave the noise of the day behind and enter a space where the only thing that matters is you.

We built Vie on a simple but powerful belief: that exceptional beauty services should feel personal, not transactional. That you should leave not just looking better, but feeling genuinely renewed. Every detail in our salon — the atmosphere, the products, the people — reflects that belief.

Our team of passionate specialists stay at the forefront of beauty trends and techniques, so you always receive treatment that is both timeless and contemporary. Whether you visit us weekly or as a rare treat, the standard never changes.
